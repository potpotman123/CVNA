Research date: 2026-10-30. Result: no matched before/after completed-vehicle output series found. No utilization estimate or forecast revised. Read staffing-test-results, ramp-evidence and source-test first. Searched site tours, local reporting, economic-development records, former-operator testimony, supplier cases and corroborating roles. No purchases, outreach or bulk scraping. Existing sources rediscovered are not new evidence.

## More useful detail extracted from a previously identified source

[In Practise, interview conducted August 23, 2023](https://inpractise.com/articles/carvana-evolution-of-the-inspection-and-reconditioning-process), questions on Blue Mound lines and restart timing: former director describes three physical lines across two shifts at peak, versus reportedly two operating line-shifts in 2023. Clarification later in the interview resolves ambiguous use of “line.” Estimated restart: about 120 days; mature performance nearer six months. These are retrospective testimony and estimates, not audited output or present capacity. Identity is hidden. Different stages share vehicles, so independent line productivity cannot be assumed. Interview also describes historical insourcing; employee counts alone may therefore miss changes in purchased labor.

Blue Mound's existing OSHA series is 756 / 400 / 398 / 523 for 2022–25. The staffing decline is directionally consistent with the testimony; annual averages cannot validate the contemporaneous shift count. Current staffed shifts and completions remain unknown. Do not treat two divided by six as measured vehicle utilization or assume threefold remaining growth.

## Additional source and denominator checks

[True Driven fleet page](https://www.thetruedriven.com/fleet) advertises detailing upwards of 25 vehicles/day for Carvana. Undated, no identified facility or worker count. This is supplier testimony, not a site output measure. It demonstrates why external labor scope should be checked before interpreting cars per Carvana employee; it cannot establish outsourcing at the three test sites.

[August 12, 2026 JPMorgan conference transcript](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/cvna/transcripts/726479-j-p-morgan-automotive-conference/), Mark Jenkins: attributes late-2025/early-2026 cost and throughput difficulties to adding 16 facilities from an 18-facility base between mid-2024 and late-2025. Describes recovering labor efficiency, then throughput, then normalized vehicle mix. Company testimony, not independent attribution; 16 additions are not independently reconciled here. It supports testing network-management execution as well as technician availability.

## Decision

The search strengthens the historical operational-headroom mechanism, especially at Blue Mound, but fails the paired-output acceptance condition. Do not fit an output/employment elasticity, annualize Winder using assumed workdays, or infer capacity from announced staffing.

Next discriminating evidence remains dated completed output and active shifts from the identified full Haines City tour reports or equivalent operating disclosures. Further generic searches or job-posting counts are unlikely to close the gap. No new data purchase is necessary to retain the supported conclusion: existing-site staffing recovered, but the amount of economically executable remaining output is not measured.
