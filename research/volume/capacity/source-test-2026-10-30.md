# Expanded evidence-source test: site utilization and executable capacity

Research date: 2026-10-30. Read the existing ramp note and transcript manifest first. Investigated all six proposed source categories plus training and collision-repair peers. Bounded web discovery followed specific municipal, practitioner and workforce leads; existing CapIQ CarMax transcripts were reused. No outreach, purchases, large-scale collection or new transcript download. CapIQ browser availability was checked; relevant additional calls were publicly accessible, so no new CapIQ navigation was needed.

## Conclusion

The evidence supports treating capacity as role- and process-specific, and shows that announced staffing is not a reliable proxy for actual staffing. It still does not quantify national unused operational capacity. The best new direct observation is Trenton employment; the best quantitative mechanism is a former operator's repair-mix example. Neither supplies paired site output and costs. A remaining gap is why individual sites have spare capacity: hiring friction, deliberate cost discipline, network allocation and insufficient profitable demand remain competing explanations.

## 1. Municipal employment records: useful, and not uniformly bullish

[Trenton weekly council report, week of August 19, 2024](https://www.ci.trenton.oh.us/DocumentCenter/View/1581/8-19-2024-Weekly-Council-Report), PDF p.2, finance director's TIRC update: approximately 150 Carvana headcount. This is a government-reported approximate observation; underlying reporting period, worker categories and FTE equivalence are unspecified.

[February 5, 2026 council packet](https://trentonoh.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_02052026-536), PDF p.5, minutes included in packet: finance director says the original 400-FTE commitment was missed. A revised agreement signed by Carvana lowered the threshold to 325, reduced the abatement from 65% to 50%, and required over $300,000 repayment, with county approval still conditional in this account. **325 is a revised commitment, not measured employment.** This demonstrates execution below an incentive plan, not proof of a technician shortage or physical utilization. Do not calculate 150/400 as factory utilization: headcount and FTE definitions, dates and production are unmatched.

Searches around Greenfield and West Memphis did not produce comparable actual-employment series in this pass. Trenton is an original IRC, not an ADESA integration. It is a useful counterexample to assuming all locations ramp similarly, not a direct ADESA ramp estimate.

## 2. Buffalo: original article located; metric still inadequate

## 5. Technical supplier and practitioner evidence

Revisited paint supplier discovery; the previously documented Tolleson equipment benchmark remains the strongest numerical supplier result. No newer independently measured ADESA before/after throughput case emerged. Existing Oracle/Accenture results remain unsuitable for direct calibration without baseline and site definitions.

[In Practise interview, conducted August 23 and published August 28, 2023](https://inpractise.com/articles/carvana-evolution-of-the-inspection-and-reconditioning-process): publicly accessible testimony from a self-described former Blue Mound director; profile hidden, identity not independently verified. Describes paint/bodywork skills and outsourcing as historical constraints. Gives a repair-mix example: 1,000 cars/week requiring an extra half-hour each creates 500 additional labor hours. This is retrospective testimony, not an audited site dataset. The stated peak Blue Mound headcount and the example's vehicle count are not matched-period measures; do not divide them to estimate productivity. Use the testimony to test mechanisms, not as current Carvana HPU.

Derived illustration: 1,000 * 0.5 = 500 hours/week; at an assumed 40 productive hours per worker-week, 500/40 = 12.5 worker-equivalents. Forty is an illustrative productive-hour assumption, not a reported shift schedule. Required paid headcount can be higher because of training, idle time and absence. This is incremental work, not a staffing recommendation.

## 6. Training and collision-repair peers

[Carvana recruitment brochure hosted by Cuyahoga Community College](https://www.tri-c.edu/programs/automotive-technology/documents/2024-1-18-carvana-recruitment-brochure1.pdf): recruits inventory, cosmetic and automotive technicians; does not disclose training completion counts or a time-to-proficiency series. Hosting recruitment material does not prove a measured training partnership. Excluded seven-week customer-advocate training from technical-labor assumptions because the role is unrelated.

[Boyd/Gerber official technician program](https://careers.boydgroup.com/us-brands/gerber-tech-dev): 18+ months with a skilled mentor to develop bodywork capability; structural training can continue afterward. This establishes that developing specialized repair labor can take much longer than basic onboarding. It is not Carvana's training duration: Carvana's retail selection avoids much severe collision work, and trainees may contribute output before graduation. A 2025 sustainability-report download was forbidden; no unverified graduate totals used.

[Boyd Q2 2026 call, August 12](https://earningscalls.dev/transcripts/boyd-group-services-inc_byd_earnings_call_transcript_2026-08-12), Brian Kaner response to Tom Wendler, segment 25: management measures technician utilization through hours per technician per week and says some headroom remains. Transcript is publicly hosted, not checked against audio/CapIQ; transcription errors are visible. No numeric utilization rate or clear billed-versus-clock-hours definition is supplied. Supports the measurement framework, not a transferable CVNA utilization percentage.

## Implication and stopping point

The simplistic bullish claim is not established: low staffing relative to a plan does not prove profitable demand is waiting, or that hiring alone unlocks output. A stronger conditional claim is that installed infrastructure can support more output if bottleneck skills, shifts and repair mix permit it, without quality losses or excessive cost.

The next useful observation would be a later actual Trenton headcount paired with production, or a defined Buffalo reconditioning count and shift history. Further broad job-posting searches would add weak proxies. No national utilization percentage, site ramp curve or incremental sales forecast is revised from this pass.
