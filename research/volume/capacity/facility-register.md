## Infrastructure and geographic interpretation

No site-level demonstrated, staffed or installed capacity was verified. Generic investment guidelines are retained at network level in the report, not imputed to each row. The JSON separately records paired original IRCs, source quality, construction evidence and unresolved dates.

Jacksonville is the independently documented substantial build: existing auction buildings remain, with a new reconditioning structure. Other auction properties may already offer wholesale repair, logistics or last-mile services; those functions do not establish Carvana retail integration. Carvana's 2025 property table combines IRCs with hubs, vending machines, dealerships and auctions, so its acreage and square footage cannot be allocated to IRC capacity.
