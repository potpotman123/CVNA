# Search for a matched paint-capacity observation

Research date: October 31, 2026. Follow-up to the [paint study](README.md).

**Result: no qualifying matched observation found.** Two additional public accounts supply useful partial measurements: staffing-sensitive inspection cadence and historical cosmetic labor per vehicle. Neither identifies present paint headroom. Keep current site headroom estimates unknown and the forecast unchanged.

## What would qualify

At minimum, one identified site and period with accepted completed output, paint workload, and available qualified labor or operational equipment hours. A second comparable period would permit a change analysis. A single snapshot would still require downtime, repair mix and downstream constraints to distinguish recoverable inefficiency from spare physical assets.

## Additional evidence

### 1. Inspection cadence changes with staffing

[Recurve, Touring Carvana’s Operations](https://recurvecap.com/insights/touring-carvanas-operations), “Highlights from the IRC.” Displays October 16; year unverified. **IRC unnamed.** A bullish investor describes inspection cadence of 13 or 6.5 minutes per vehicle depending on staffing, illustrating two versus four tire-station workers. It also reports wholesale routing above eight painted panels. These are historical tour claims, not verified current policy.

Derived nominal lane rates: `60/13 = 4.615` and `60/6.5 = 9.231` vehicles/hour. This supports staffing-driven inspection throughput, not doubled labor productivity or paint output. Missing: paint hours, workload, accepted releases and downtime. Do not assign the IRC to Haines City or Los Angeles.

### 2. A historical cosmetic-labor estimate, not a paint cycle

[In Practise, Carvana Operations: Vehicle Flow through the Network](https://inpractise.com/articles/carvana-unit-flow), interview October 10, published October 19, 2023; answers about process duration.

The interviewee estimates approximately two mechanical labor-hours and 1.5–2 cosmetic labor-hours per vehicle. Cosmetics combines painting, paintless dent repair and interior work. Site, averaging population, period and rework treatment are unspecified; the profile is hidden. Independence from the August interview is unverified.

**Use:** historical testimony for sensitivity design. **Exclude from calibration:** current paint labor, booth occupancy and staffing. Combining this estimate with Elyria’s 2026 output would create an unsupported matched observation.

### 3. Repeat visits still lack a matched output denominator

[Recurve, How Fast Can Carvana Grow?](https://recurvecap.com/insights/how-fast-can-carvana-grow), March 11, context identifies 2025, section on Haines City. The author visited in November 2023 and March 2025 and describes increasing digitization. However, its numerical production series uses Clarity Markets estimates for the network, not measured Haines City output. Those estimates were already recorded in the staffing research. **No site-level before/after productivity calculation is supported.**

## Research decision

The important distinction is **buying more throughput with more labor versus producing more with the same resources**. The new inspection account supports the first mechanism at one unidentified facility; it cannot quantify the second. Nor does an inspection-stage improvement establish that paint or final release can absorb the faster arrivals.

Before increasing the model’s output assumption, obtain one consistently defined observation containing: site and week; accepted releases; share and severity of paint jobs; available painter and booth hours; repeat-work hours; and whether vehicles queue before or after painting. A comparable second observation would help separate staffing, mix and execution. This is a measurement specification, not authorization for outreach or a paid collection project.

The pass checked prior notes and supplied tours, then public practitioner transcripts, firsthand investor visits, supplier/operating accounts and selected facility searches. Followed concrete leads, without purchases, outreach, bulk scraping or repeating the failed registration/permit searches. Further generic discovery was returning advertisements and already catalogued claims, so expansion stopped. No new physical-capacity, paint-headroom or sales parameter enters the model.
