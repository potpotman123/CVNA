# Staffing recovery after 2022: company history and site evidence

**Follow-up:** [Official OSHA panel and test results](staffing-test-results-2026-10-30.md) resolves the download gap below and measures staffing at 14 matched IRCs. This earlier note is retained as discovery history; the follow-up supersedes its statement that no raw OSHA panel was available.

Research date: 2026-10-30. Latest verified absolute company headcount: December 2025, not Q3 2026. Broader discovery covered filings, municipal incentive compliance, local reporting, issuer bond documents, OSHA establishment data, publicly published investor research and job/profile datasets. No paid data or outreach.

## Result

Reported company employment increased approximately 39.2% from year-end 2022 to year-end 2025, and 68.6% from the lower year-end 2023 base. Staffing restoration is consistent with executable capacity expanding inside an existing footprint. It does not identify how much production growth came from staffing, or establish remaining profitable demand. A large recovery has already occurred.

## New source worth pursuing: OSHA annual establishment filings

[Official ITA data](https://www.osha.gov/itadata) and [summary dictionary](https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/summary_data_dictionary.pdf) offer establishment identifiers/addresses, annual average employees and hours worked. This is better suited to staffing measurement than LinkedIn/profile coverage or vacancy counts. Counts are employer-reported, not OSHA-validated, and coverage depends on reporting criteria.

The official 2024 ZIP download was blocked by Chrome (ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT). No workaround or security changes were attempted. Raw records were not extracted. Therefore this research does NOT yet contain a verified OSHA staffing panel.

Secondary publication PlainSafetyScore exposes Carvana records with approximate staffing labels: [Haines City](https://plainsafetyscore.com/employer/carvana-haines-city-fl), [Chesterfield](https://plainsafetyscore.com/employer/carvana-chesterfield-va), [Heath](https://plainsafetyscore.com/employer/carvana-heath-oh), [Elyria](https://plainsafetyscore.com/employer/carvana-elyria-oh), [Oklahoma City](https://plainsafetyscore.com/employer/carvana-oklahoma-city-ok), [San Antonio](https://plainsafetyscore.com/employer/carvana-san-antonio-tx). The publisher mixes employee/FTE language and includes multi-year summaries; do not import its headline figures as annual headcounts without checking underlying rows. Injury grades are irrelevant to this staffing test and are not adopted.

## Identification and model treatment

Match each facility by address, then separate recon from auction/logistics functions and original IRCs from ADESA conversions. Obtain annual average staffing AND paid/worked hours; more overtime can raise output without higher headcount. Match completed retail-ready vehicles over identical periods, not vehicles stored, inspected, auctioned or sold to nearby residents. Cars can be shipped nationally.

Useful measures: completed retail units / recon labor hours; recon labor hours / average recon employees; active lines/shifts relative to installed lines. Site headcount / advertised future employment is not utilization.

Discriminating observations: staffing/hours rising before production supports an enabling role; output rising without hours points toward productivity/mix; staffing rising without output points toward ramp inefficiency, workload or demand problems. Common demand can drive both hiring and production, so correlation alone cannot establish causation. Control vehicle repair intensity and compare established sites with expansion sites where possible.

**Can enter model:** company employment history as context. **Provisional case evidence:** Trenton's decline/rebound and Chesterfield's staged line/staffing plan. **Cannot yet enter forecast:** site output elasticity to staffing, utilization, unused skilled capacity, or aggregate growth attributable to rehiring. The exact next evidence gap is the raw OSHA panel plus matched site production—not another traffic/sales correlation.
