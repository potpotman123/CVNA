# ADESA regional evidence check

As of 2026-10-29. Free sources only. Conclusion: production expansion is a plausible contributor to growth; the available regional comparison cannot identify its causal contribution, isolate ADESA, or estimate conversion elasticity.

The top/bottom gap is 40 percentage points of production growth and 24 points of sales growth. The ratio 24/40 = 0.6 is an arithmetic slope between two aggregated observations, not a causal elasticity or forecasting rule. The middle/bottom comparison is +22 points of production growth for +4 points of sales growth; top/middle is +18 for +20. A stable linear rule is unsupported. Production is a flow of prepared vehicles; inventory is a stock. These figures cannot establish inventory turns or whether unsold stock accumulated.

## Mechanism and competing explanations

The proposed mechanism is more local prepared vehicles → greater suitable selection and shorter delivery → higher conversion → better advertising economics → more traffic and sales. Management explicitly describes marketing reallocating toward higher-conversion regions. Consequently, even if the mechanism is correct, observed growth combines production, conversion and advertising rather than isolating one lever.

Competing explanations remain: capacity allocated to already-strong demand; regional differences in affordability/vehicle mix and financing; base effects; existing IRC expansion alongside ADESA; national inventory and logistics spillovers. Three grouped observations from one quarter, selected by production growth, with no regional pre-trends, absolute weights, advertising, pricing or consumer-demand controls cannot distinguish these explanations. The conference presentation repeats company data and is not independent validation.

## Q3 2026 update and economics

The [Q3 letter](https://investors.carvana.com/~/media/Files/C/Carvana-IR/documents/cvna-shareholder-letter-q3-2026.pdf), available locally in `work/loan-sale-economics/sources/cvna-q3-2026-letter.txt`, reports 217,385 retail units (+39% YoY), 21 integrated ADESA sites, and three full buildouts expected to produce in 2027. Two-year improvements include twice the selection and delivery two days faster; these are national management statements, not a regional causal test. No comparable new regional growth table was located in this letter.

Advertising increased from $102m to $152m YoY (+49.0%), faster than unit growth. Total GPU fell $365 to $6,997, while adjusted EBITDA increased from $637m to $800m (+25.6%). Thus volume growth continued with rising aggregate earnings, but unit economics softened. This does not isolate ADESA returns: financing, vehicle mix and other operating factors also matter.

## Forecast use and next discriminator

Use production/distribution expansion as an explicit scenario driver and monitor realized throughput, selection, delivery, advertising per unit and profitability together. Do not apply the 0.6 slope, extrapolate the top group's 54% growth nationally, or multiply site count by assumed output without ramp/utilization assumptions. Inventory-aging risk remains unresolved.

The strongest next free test is a national quarterly bridge across production/selection, unit sales, advertising per unit and gross profit, using existing filings and letters to check whether acceleration requires progressively more advertising or weaker per-unit economics. It can constrain sustainable-growth scenarios even without independent local data. A local causal estimate remains unavailable.
